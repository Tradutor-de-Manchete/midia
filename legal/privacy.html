Privacy Policy

Tradutor de Manchete Publisher - last updated November 18, 2026

1. Scope

Tradutor de Manchete Publisher is an internal tool that publishes the Tradutor de Manchete account's own videos to its own TikTok profile. It is used only by the account owners and has no public users.

2. Data the app handles

  • The OAuth access token and refresh token that TikTok issues when the account owner authorizes the app.
  • Basic information about the authorized account returned by TikTok (open ID, display name and posting settings), used only to publish.
  • The videos and captions the team chooses to publish.

The app does not collect data from other TikTok users, viewers or followers, and does not use any data for advertising or profiling.

3. Storage and sharing

Tokens are stored only on the team's own computer, in a protected configuration file, and are never shared, sold or published. Videos and captions are sent only to TikTok, through the official API.

4. Retention and removal

Tokens are kept while the app is in use. The account owner can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app settings (Security - Manage app permissions); the team then deletes the stored tokens.

5. Changes

This policy may be updated; the current version is always available at this address.

6. Contact

Send a direct message to @tradutordemanchete on Instagram or TikTok.
